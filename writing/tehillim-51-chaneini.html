Tehillim 51: Chaneini, the Psalm of Teshuvah

19 Nov 2026 / 9 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

Tehillim 51 is the psalm David said after Natan the prophet came to him. The Radak puts it exactly: David confessed to Natan, “but this prayer he said after Natan had gone to his house.”

The psalm is short and carries a great deal. It gives us the verse that opens every Amidah, “Hashem, open my lips,” the verse on the broken heart that the Sages placed above all offerings, and the words of confession that became the Rambam’s laws of teshuvah.

The booklet reads the psalm verse by verse with Rashi, the Radak, Ibn Ezra, the Metzudas David, the Malbim, the Meiri, the Alshich, the Midrash Tehillim and the Targum. It follows the psalm into the Rambam’s Hilchos Teshuvah, the Tanya on the broken heart and on joy, and the Tikkun Chatzos. The commentaries are printed in the original at the back.

The Sages teach that “whoever says David sinned is nothing but mistaken” (Shabbos 56a). The booklet keeps that teaching beside the plain confession of the psalm, and does not retell the story.
