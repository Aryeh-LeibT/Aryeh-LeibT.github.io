4.89 Million by Six O'Clock

The 18:00 turnout on election day, counted in people, and what Yitzchak did toward evening.

Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren · Jerusalem · Tuesday 27 October 2026 / 16 Cheshvan 5787

At 16:00 it was 4,085,075. At 18:00 the Central Elections Committee put out its next number: 66.7 percent of eligible voters had voted. On the roll of 7,331,981, that comes to about 4,890,431 people. At the same hour in the 2022 election it was 57.7 percent.

The day so far, as the committee reported it: 16.68 percent at 10:00, 31.5 percent at 12:00, 44.9 percent at 14:00, 55.7 percent at 16:00 and 66.7 percent at 18:00.

Once more, in people. The committee lists 7,331,981 eligible voters for this election.

  • 66.7 percent of 7,331,981 is 4,890,431. The committee gave the percentage. It hadn’t put out an exact count for 18:00 when I wrote this, so the people figures here come from the percentage and are close, not exact.
  • At 2022’s pace, 57.7 percent of today’s roll, about 4,230,600 people would have voted by 18:00 (57.7 percent of 7,331,981 is 4,230,553).
  • 4,890,431 minus 4,230,553 is 659,878. That’s about 660,000 more people than the old pace.
  • In 2022 the committee counted 3,919,367 voters by 18:00. Today is about 971,000 ahead of that. Part of the difference is a bigger roll.
  • Between 16:00 and 18:00, 4,890,431 minus 4,085,075 is 805,356 people. That’s 11.0 points in two hours. In 2022 the same two hours added 10.2.
  • And 7,331,981 minus 4,890,431 is 2,441,550. At 18:00, about 2,441,600 eligible voters still had four hours.

These are counts of who showed up. They say nothing about whom anyone chose.

This morning was Avraham rising early, and this afternoon was Avraham in the heat of the day. Next week’s parsha gives us his son toward evening. “Yitzchak went out to speak in the field toward evening” (Bereishit 24:63). The Gemara learns from that verse that Yitzchak established the afternoon prayer, Mincha (Berachot 26b).

The day’s last hours got a prayer of their own.

About 4.89 million people have voted. If you’re one of the roughly 2.44 million who haven’t yet, the evening is still yours.

Voting ends at 22:00. Whoever is in line at the ballot box by 22:00 votes. Bring your ID.

I encourage all eligible voters to vote.
