Tehillim 144: Who Trains My Hands

12 Nov 2026 / 2 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

On 11 November 2026, the first of Kislev, I finished a 105-page study booklet on Tehillim 144. Most people who pray know its last verse by heart without knowing where it comes from. Three times a day, before Ashrei, the siddur has us say: "Happy the people who have it so; happy the people whose G-d is the L-rd."

The downloadable excerpt is the introduction and Chapter One. Before David says a word about enemies or dangers, he blesses G-d for hands that were taught to fight. Midrash Tehillim, reading the opening with the verse "In all your ways acknowledge Him" (Mishlei 3:6), describes a man who has kingship, strength, wealth and victory, and who will not boast of any of them.

The full psalm is printed at the end of the excerpt.
