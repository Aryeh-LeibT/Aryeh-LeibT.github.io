Tehillim 94: The Song of the Fourth Day

12 Nov 2026 / 2 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

On 11 November 2026, a Wednesday and the first of Kislev, the psalm of the fourth day was Tehillim 94. I wrote a 90-page study booklet on it, verse by verse, with the classic commentaries.

The downloadable excerpt is the introduction and Chapter Seven, on verses 20 and 21. The psalm has already asked how long the wicked will exult. Now it names a darker thing: a throne that frames mischief by statute, wrong written into the law itself.

The commentators read those verses with care. Rashi, Radak, the Metzudas and the Malbim each take the throne a different way, and the chapter sets them beside the decrees of Chanukah and Parashas Toldos, read on Shabbos, 14 November 2026. The full psalm is printed at the end of the excerpt.

What do we do when the wrong has been written into law?
