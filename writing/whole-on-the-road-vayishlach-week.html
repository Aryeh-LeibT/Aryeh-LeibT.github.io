Whole on the Road: Vayishlach Week

21 Nov 2026 / 12 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

This booklet is for the week of Parshat Vayishlach in the Chabad daily cycle, from Sunday 22 November 2026 (12 Kislev 5787), when the daily Chumash opens Vayishlach, to Shabbat 28 November (18 Kislev), when the whole parsha is read in shul. That night begins Yud-Tes Kislev, the Rosh Hashanah of Chassidus.

It goes through the parsha one aliyah a day, the way the daily Chumash is learned, with Rashi, the Midrash and a teaching from the Rebbe where the sources give one, and the full verses in Hebrew and English. Then comes the haftarah, Chazon Ovadiah, as Chabad reads it. Ovadiah hid a hundred prophets, fifty to a cave, and one view in the Gemara (Sanhedrin 39b) says he learned it from Yaakov, who split his camp in two in the first aliyah of this parsha.

The week’s Chitas follows day by day: Tehillim 66 to 89, the close of the Tanya in Kuntres Acharon, and the Hayom Yom, with the Rambam schedule. I checked all of it against Chabad.org’s daily study pages. The booklet ends with Yud-Tes Kislev, and with questions, a story and a niggun for the Shabbat table.
