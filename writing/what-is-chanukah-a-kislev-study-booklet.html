What Is Chanukah? A Kislev Study Booklet

12 Nov 2026 / 2 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

On 11 November 2026, the first of Kislev, I finished a 92-page study booklet on the month we had just entered. It is not the short essay I posted on 10 November 2026, which is already on this site as Kislev as the Architecture of Completion. It tests that essay against the sources, chapter by chapter.

The downloadable excerpt is the introduction and Chapter Five. In the middle of the laws of wicks and oils, the Gemara stops and asks a question it asks of no other festival: "What is Chanukah?"

The war is told in a single clause. The oil is told in full. As the Gemara says, "The following year they fixed them and made them festive days," once the meaning had settled. The Rambam, writing law, tells the history more fully, including the turn "until the G-d of our fathers had mercy on them."

The full booklet has nine chapters and six appendices. This file holds only the introduction and that Gemara chapter.
