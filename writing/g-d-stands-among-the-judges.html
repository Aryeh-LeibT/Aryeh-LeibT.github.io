G-d Stands Among the Judges: A Tehillim 82 Booklet

10 Nov 2026 / 30 Cheshvan 5787, Rosh Chodesh Kislev

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

Tehillim 82 is the psalm of the third day, and it opens in a courtroom: "G-d stands in the assembly." The judges are judged first.

I've put together a full booklet on it, 96 pages, verse by verse, with the classic commentaries and a bibliography. It asks what the psalm demands of anyone who sits in judgment, and what it asks of the rest of us who live under their rulings.

Read it slowly, share it with your study partner, and tell me where I got it wrong.
