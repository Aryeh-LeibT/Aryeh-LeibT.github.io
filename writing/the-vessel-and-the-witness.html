The Vessel and the Witness: Torah from Bereishis to Sukkos

10 Nov 2026 / 30 Cheshvan 5787, Rosh Chodesh Kislev

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

A study booklet for the Shabbos table and the beis midrash: The Vessel and the Witness, now in a second, fuller edition. Eight chapters walk from Bereishis to Sukkos with maamarim and sichos of the Lubavitcher Rebbe and my own essays. Each chapter now adds Rashi, Ramban, the Midrash, the haftaros and Tehillim, each in Hebrew with a working translation and questions for study. Every quotation was checked against its source.

All glory to the Ribono shel Olam · Errors are mine
