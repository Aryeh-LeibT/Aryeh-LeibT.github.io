5.45 Million by Eight O'Clock

Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

By 20:00, 74.3% of eligible Israelis had voted, compared with 66% at the same hour in 2022. That's 5,449,277 people, according to the Central Elections Committee.

About 560,000 of them voted in the two hours after 18:00. With two hours still to go, turnout is already past the 2022 final of 70.63%.

Voting ends at 22:00. If you're in line by 22:00, you vote. Bring your ID.

I encourage all eligible voters to vote.
