One Slip, Blue Pen

What to bring, what not to write, and the rule that matters if the line is long. A printable card for the last hours of voting, Tuesday 27 October 2026 / 16 Cheshvan 5787.

Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

Voting ends at 22:00 tonight. In small communities of 350 voters or fewer, and in hospitals, prisons and care homes, it ends at 20:00.

If you're in line by 22:00, you vote. The Central Elections Committee's acting director-general said today that if there's a line at 22:00, stations keep working until everyone who arrived by 22:00 has voted. So don't turn around at the door because the line looks long.

What to bring

Bring one of these:

  • Your Israeli ID card. An expired card is fine if the photo still shows it's you.
  • A valid Israeli driver's licence with your photo.
  • A valid Israeli passport.
  • A valid IDF personal ID card.

A photo of your ID on your phone won't be accepted. The voter notice that came in the mail isn't ID, and you don't need it to vote.

No ID at all? Ten Population Authority offices are open today, 08:00 to 18:00, to issue a temporary ID for voting. Bring two passport photos. The offices are in Jerusalem, Tel Aviv, Rishon LeZion, Kfar Saba, Bnei Brak, Be'er Sheva, Ashkelon, the Krayot, Nof HaGalil and Tzfat.

Where

You vote only at the station you're assigned to. If you don't know where it is, the Interior Ministry's locator line is 1-800-101-975.

Behind the screen

  • Take one printed slip and put it in the envelope. Don't add anything to it: no writing, no signature, no marks.
  • If your list's slip has run out, ask for a blank slip. Write the list's letters on it in blue pen. A blank slip left empty doesn't count.
  • Two or three identical slips in one envelope count as one vote. Four or more, or slips for different lists, and the envelope is void.
  • Don't mark the envelope. You don't have to seal it.
  • If something's wrong at the station, tell the station committee.

Getting there

Israel Railways trains and longer intercity bus routes are free until midnight today, and you don't need ID to ride. Check your route before you leave.

The math on the line

There are 7,331,981 eligible voters and 12,018 ballot boxes. That's 7,331,981 ÷ 12,018, or about 610 voters per box on average. If every one of them shows up in the evening, you get a line. That's why the 22:00 rule matters.

Why bother

Section II of the Covenantal Republicanism monograph starts from a simple idea. A free people chooses who governs it, and stays answerable for that choice. Today that choice is one slip in one envelope.

I encourage all eligible voters to vote.

Voting ends at 22:00.
