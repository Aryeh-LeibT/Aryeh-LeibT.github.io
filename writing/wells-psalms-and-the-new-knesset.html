Wells, Psalms and the New Knesset: A 30 Cheshvan Booklet

10 Nov 2026 / 30 Cheshvan 5787, Rosh Chodesh Kislev

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

On 10 November 2026, Rosh Chodesh Kislev, the 26th Knesset took its oath that afternoon. Parashas Toldos, read on Shabbos, 14 November 2026, is where Yitzchak reopens his father's wells and keeps the old names.

This booklet gathers fifty threads from those days into thirteen chapters of about a hundred pages: the wells of Toldot, the psalms we say every morning, and a new Knesset finding its feet. The sources are cited in full in the bibliography.

Take a chapter a night.
