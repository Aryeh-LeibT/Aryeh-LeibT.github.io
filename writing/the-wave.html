3. I'm inside the water (poem)

I'm inside the water,

And I don't know where I've gone,

The sky has lost its shape above,

The shore has come undone.

I'm inside the water,

And the wave is all I see,

A world of moving shadows

Closing in around me.

I reach for something steady,

For a rock, a rope, a sign,

But everything I try to hold

Slips through these hands of mine.

I'm inside the water,

And my lungs are burning now,

Searching through the chaos

For a way, a where, a how.

I break the surface briefly,

Steal a breath and sink again,

Caught between the fear of now

And not knowing where I've been.

The panic comes in flashes,

Sharp as lightning in the dark,

Telling me the ocean

Has already left its mark.

Telling me I'm helpless,

Telling me to let it win,

Telling me this endless sea

Will never let me in.

But somewhere underneath it,

Beneath the fear and noise,

A quieter tide is moving,

A softer kind of voice.

It says, "You do not need to

Cross the ocean all at once.

You do not need the answers.

You do not need to run."

"Find the next breath waiting.

Find the air that's closest by.

You don't have to reach the shoreline.

You only have to try."

So I'm inside the water,

And the wave is still immense,

But I am learning slowly

Not to measure my defense

By whether I can stop it,

Or command the sea to part,

But by how I keep on breathing

When it's breaking through my heart.

One breath when the wave rises.

One breath when I can't see.

One breath when fear is shouting.

One breath belongs to me.

And though the water takes me

Farther than I want to go,

The current cannot take away

The things I've come to know:

That every sea has limits.

That every storm will tire.

That even when I'm trembling,

There is something that survives.

So I'm inside the water,

And I'm frightened, this is true.

But I'm still here beneath the wave,

And somehow making it through.
