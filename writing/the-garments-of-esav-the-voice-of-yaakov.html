The Garments of Esav, the Voice of Yaakov

What the hands wore, what the voice said, and which one Yitzchak trusted.

12 Nov 2026 / 2 Kislev 5787

By Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren

Yitzchak can't see. He hears Yaakov's voice, feels Esav's hands, and then he smells the garments (Bereishit 27:27). The Gemara (Sanhedrin 37a) reads בגדיו, "his garments," as bogdav, "his betrayers." Rashi says the scent was the smell of Gan Eden. Even the people who may someday walk away still carry something worth blessing.

That's a hard lesson for anyone who leads a team, a family or a community. Judge by the voice, which means what a person learns, says and builds. A role, a title or a borrowed coat is not the person. Chazal promise that when the voice of Yaakov is strong, the hands of Esav lose their power (Bereishit Rabbah 65:20).

From Pittsburgh grit to Jerusalem emunah, I keep coming back to this: strengthen the voice, and let G-d handle the garments.
