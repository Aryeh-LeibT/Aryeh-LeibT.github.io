The podium’s favorite confusion

The confusion is always the same. A hall of delegations is asked to behave like a tribunal. “Genocidal mindset,” “ethnic cleansing,” “concentration camp,” “expansionist appetite” — these are courtroom words when they are used as if a finding had been entered. On a podium they are oratory. Oratory can be true, false, mixed, or merely useful to the speaker’s home audience. The republic that still has to govern is not required to pretend that oratory is a judgment.
