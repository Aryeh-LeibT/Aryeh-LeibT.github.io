Elections 2026

Israel votes for the 26th Knesset on Tuesday 27 October 2026. This page gathers my writing on the campaign in one place: the polls, the arithmetic of a 61-seat majority, the lists and alliances, and the two stories from outside the campaign that keep pressing in on it. Where an election fact matters, I go by the Central Elections Committee record. Where something is not yet confirmed, I say so.

Polls

Polls are a snapshot, not a forecast. I read them in blocs, not headlines: who is near 61, who is not, and which parties decide the difference. In recent weeks neither bloc has reached 61, and the Arab parties hold the balance.

  • The 61 That Isn’t There

Coalition arithmetic

A Knesset majority is 61 of 120 seats. Seats come from votes, but governments come from partners, and partners come with red lines. These pieces work through who can sit with whom, and what each path to 61 would cost.
