Torah: Tishrei & Bereishit

Tishrei is the month that carries the whole year. It moves from the shofar of Rosh Hashanah, through the white of Yom Kippur, into the sukkah, and on to the dancing of Simchat Torah. Then, without a pause, the scroll rolls back to its first word and we begin again with Bereishit. These pieces walk that road, from the days of judgment to the days of dwelling, and on into the weekly parsha.
