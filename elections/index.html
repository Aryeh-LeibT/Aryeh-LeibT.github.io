How we work: official election bodies first. Every number links to its source and shows when we retrieved it. We cite polls one at a time and don't copy anyone's average. Anything resting on one source is marked as single-source. Projections are labelled as ours, and each comes with its equations.

The tracker does not update on Shabbat or Yom Tov, results night included. It catches up after Havdalah.
