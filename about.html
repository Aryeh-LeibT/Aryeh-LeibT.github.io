About

I was born in 1985 in San Diego, and my family moved to Pittsburgh in 1987. I am a Steelers, Pirates, and Penguins fan. We made Aliyah in 1996. I live in Jerusalem with my wife and five children.

I have a BA in Middle Eastern Studies and Political Science from Ariel University, and an MA in Political Science from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

What I write sits at the meeting of Jewish political thought and republican government. The featured pieces are on the Writing page.
