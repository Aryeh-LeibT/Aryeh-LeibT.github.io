About

Shalom, and welcome. I’m Aryeh Leib Thorne Toren, and I write from Jerusalem.

My writing moves between three places that, for me, have never really been separate: the Torah, the State of Israel, and the region we live in. Some weeks that means a parsha essay for the Shabbat table, or a piece on the festivals of Tishrei. Other weeks it means working through the arithmetic of an Israeli election, or the pressure between Washington and Tehran, or what a single flight can teach us about courage and partnership.

I try to write plainly, to source what I say, and to keep what is confirmed apart from what is not. I write for readers in Israel and across the Diaspora, and I hope each piece leaves you with something worth bringing home.
