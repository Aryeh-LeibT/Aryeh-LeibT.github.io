About

I am a Jerusalem-based Torah scholar, researcher, and writer. Pittsburgh roots. I was born in 1985 in San Diego; my family moved to Pittsburgh in 1987. Steelers, Pirates, Penguins. We made Aliyah in 1996.

I have a BA in Middle Eastern Studies and Political Science from Ariel University, and an MA in Political Science from the Hebrew University of Jerusalem.

The essays join Orthodox Torah and Chassidus with republican political thought (Pettit, Nelson, Elazar, Hazony). I write as a talmid, not a consultant, and I write G-d, not God. Featured pieces are on the Writing page. CV (PDF).
